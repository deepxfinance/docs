title: Risk Controls
---

DeepX uses risk controls to limit the effect of large positions on initial margin.

## Large Position Penalty

[Large Position Penalty](/glossary#large-position-penalty) adjusts an initial margin weight when the post-action notional value exceeds its threshold.

| Activity | Adjusted Weight | Effect Above Threshold |
|----------|-----------------|------------------------|
| Perpetual position | IMR | Increases required initial margin |
| Lending deposit | IAW | Reduces usable initial collateral |
| Lending borrow | IBW | Increases initial liability |

The adjustment is continuous rather than a step change at the threshold. It does not affect MMR, MAW, or MBW.
