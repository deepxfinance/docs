DeepDex offers a dynamic and transparent trading environment for perpetual futures. To navigate it effectively, traders should understand the key components that shape market behavior and risk management.

## Spot Market

At the heart of DeepDex's perpetual market is a price feed known as the Index Price, which reflects the fair spot market value of the asset. 

Trading volume over the past 24 hours offers insight into how active the market is, while the market status indicates whether trading is live or paused.

### Trading Rules

DeepDex charges a taker fee on executed trades. For makers, the fee structure is dynamic—positive values indicate fees paid, while negative values reflect rebates earned. Spot trading fees are charged in the asset you receive from the trade, while perpetual trading fees are charged in USDC.

The platform uses three key parameters to control trade precision:
- Tick Size: This defines the smallest price increment in an order. For example, if the tick size for BTC/USDC is 0.1 USDC, the price must be a multiple of 0.1 USDC per BTC.
- Minimum Order Size: This sets the smallest allowable trade. For example, if the minimum order size for BTC/USDC is 0.00001 BTC, you cannot place an order for less than 0.00001 BTC.
- Minimum Notional: This specifies the minimum total value of an order. For example, if the minimum notional for BTC/USDC is 10 USDC, you cannot place an order that is worth less than 1 USDC in total.
- Step Size: This determines the smallest increment by which trade quantities can be adjusted. For example, if the step size for BTC/USDC is 0.00001 BTC, you cannot place an order for a quantity that is not a multiple of 0.00001 BTC.

Together, these parameters help maintain a clean, efficient trading environment while allowing users to execute trades with precision.

Liquidation incentives depend on how the liquidation is executed. An orderbook liquidator receives the Orderbook Liquidator Fee on the executed notional value. A liquidator that takes over a position or spot asset instead receives the fixed Takeover Reward through its takeover price: a long position or spot asset is transferred at `Mark Price × (1 − Takeover Reward)`, while a short position is transferred at `Mark Price × (1 + Takeover Reward)`. These two incentives do not apply to the same liquidation. The Liquidation Insurance Fee is charged separately and allocated to the Insurance Fund.

## Perpetual Market

In addition to the spot market, perpetual market maintains a P&L Pool to absorb profits and losses, and an Insurance Fund to cover deficits from liquidations. 

DeepDex Perpetual uses a Funding Rate mechanism to balance long and short positions. When demand skews heavily in one direction, funding payments incentivize traders to take the opposite side. This rate updates periodically and reflects recent market activity.

There are two parameters that control a market's leverage level:
- [Initial Margin Ratio (IMR)](/glossary/#initial-margin-ratio-imr): The percentage of a new perpetual position's value required as collateral to open the position.
- [Maintenance Margin Ratio (MMR)](/glossary/#maintenance-margin-ratio-mmr): The minimum percentage of the perpetual position value that must be maintained as collateral to keep the position open.
