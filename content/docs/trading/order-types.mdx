---
title: Order Types
---

## Market Order

Market orders execute immediately against the available order book at the best available prices. Any unfilled quantity is canceled.

## Limit Order

Limit orders execute only at the price you set or a better price. They remain on the order book until they are filled or canceled.

## Immediate or Cancel (IOC)

IOC orders execute immediately for any available quantity. Any unfilled quantity is canceled, and the order never remains on the order book.

## Reduce-Only Order

Reduce-only orders can decrease or close an existing perpetual position, but cannot increase it or open a position in the opposite direction.

## Post-Only Order

Post-only limit orders add liquidity to the order book. If an order would execute immediately, it is rejected instead of taking existing liquidity.
