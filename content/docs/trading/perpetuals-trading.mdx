title: Perpetuals Trading

A [Perpetual Contract](/glossary#perpetual-contract) tracks the price of an underlying asset with no expiry date, allowing you to trade in either direction with leverage.

## Long and Short

- **Long**: You profit when the price rises.
- **Short**: You profit when the price falls.

Opening a position requires [Initial Margin](/glossary#initial-margin) as collateral. Your maximum position size is limited by the market's maximum [Leverage](/trading/leverage) and your account's initial-margin ratio.

## Opening a Position

1. [Deposit](/getting-started/deposit-withdraw-borrow-repay) assets as collateral, or use assets you already hold on DeepX.
2. On the perpetual trading page, pick a market and your order type. See [Order Types](/trading/order-types).
3. Set the quantity and price. The maximum notional is your available margin times your effective [Leverage](/trading/leverage#on-chain-leverage) cap.
4. Submit the order. It executes against the order book, and any filled quantity becomes your position.

Your position's margin requirement and liquidation distance follow the formulas in [Leverage](/trading/leverage). While it stays open, it accrues [Funding Rates](/trading/funding-rates) every hour.

## Closing a Position

Place an opposite order to reduce or close your position. A [Reduce-Only Order](/trading/order-types#reduce-only-order) guarantees the fill cannot increase or flip your position. Alternatively, lower your leverage cap never closes existing positions—only new increases are blocked.

Unrealized PnL stays floating until closed or settled. See [PnL Settlement](#pnl-settlement) below.

## PnL Settlement

PnL settlement converts an open perpetual position's [Unrealized PnL](/glossary#unrealized-profit-and-loss-unrealized-pnl) and unsettled funding payments into USDC deposits or borrows.

- Profits increase the USDC deposit. They earn interest and can be used as margin.
- Losses first reduce the USDC deposit. Any shortfall becomes a USDC borrow that accrues interest.

Settlement does not change an account's [uniMMR](/glossary#unified-maintenance-margin-ratio-unimmr): USDC has 100% asset and borrow weights, so converting floating items into deposits or borrows leaves the collateral ratio unchanged. A new borrow starts accruing interest afterward, gradually lowering uniMMR over time.

### Settlement Reference Price

After each settlement, the position's settlement reference price is updated to the current [Mark Price](/glossary#mark-price). The next settlement's unrealized PnL is measured from this new reference:

```math
\text{UnrealizedPnl} = (\text{MarkPrice} - \text{SettlementReferencePrice}) \times \text{PositionSize}
```

Unsettled funding is tracked from a per-position checkpoint (the cumulative funding index at entry or at the last settlement) and is settled together with unrealized PnL.

### Position Changes

Funding is settled before any position size change. Increases update the entry and settlement reference prices by the weighted fill price; decreases leave them unchanged; a flip closes the existing position and opens the opposite one as a new position.

### When Settlement Happens

Settlement is not forced on a schedule. Anyone can settle any subaccount at any time. The platform periodically settles losing positions, while users may settle profitable positions themselves. Until a settlement happens, floating losses still count toward collateral value, so uniMMR remains accurate.
