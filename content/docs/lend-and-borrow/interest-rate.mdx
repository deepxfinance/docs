## Borrow Interest Rate

The borrow interest rate for the DeepX lending protocol is determined by a multi-linear model with a minimum rate, zero to five kink points, and a maximum rate at 100% utilization. [Utilization](/glossary#utilization) is `borrows / (availableLiquidity + borrows)`, where `availableLiquidity = cash - lockedLiquidity`. For each asset, the following parameters are defined:

- Minimum rate $R_\text{min}$
- Utilization rates ($U_1$, $U_2$, $U_3$, etc.) and the corresponding interest rates ($R_1$, $R_2$, $R_3$, etc.) at zero to five kinks
- Maximum rate $R_\text{max}$ at 100% utilization

Between adjacent points, the borrow rate is interpolated linearly. With no kinks, it interpolates directly from $R_\text{min}$ at zero utilization to $R_\text{max}$ at full utilization.

## Supply Interest Rate

The supply interest rate is derived from the borrow rate, utilization ($U$), and the [reserve factor](/glossary#reserve-factor) ($\rho$):

```math
R_\text{supply} = R_\text{borrow} \times U \times (1 - \rho)
```
