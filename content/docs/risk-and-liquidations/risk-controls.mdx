title: Risk Controls
---

DeepX uses risk controls to limit the effect of concentrated lending pools on initial margin.

## Concentration Asset Discount (CAD)

[Concentration Asset Discount (CAD)](/glossary#concentration-asset-discount-cad) reduces the [Initial Asset Weight (IAW)](/glossary#initial-asset-weight-iaw) of an asset for every account when deposits in its lending pool become concentrated.

The CAD threshold is a pool's Supply Cap multiplied by its CAD value. Above this threshold, CAD reduces IAW proportionally as total deposits increase. CAD does not affect MAW, IBW, or MBW. Because IAW is used for initial margin checks, it does not increase maintenance requirements for existing positions.
